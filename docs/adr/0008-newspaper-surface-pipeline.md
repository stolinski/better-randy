# `newspaper` Surface Pipeline + `SurfaceRenderer.shaderPass`

> Compose-pipeline invocation resolved by [ADR-0010](0010-compose-pipeline-shaderpass-invocation.md). The `newspaper-physics` shader (halftone + ink bleed) now executes between the surface's DOM upload and the final composite.

The torn-newspaper-clipping aesthetic the channel claims (warm aged paper, halftone dot screen at body sizes, ink bleed at glyph edges, condensed serif body, heavy slab / bold-serif display) cannot be expressed as a variant of the existing `paper` Surface — `paper`'s substrate is a clean light-cream sheet whose shader bakes laid-paper grain and isotropic warmth, neither of which produce the newsprint physics. We added `newspaper` as its own Surface Pipeline with its own CanvasSource (warm-white substrate `#f0e8d6`, condensed serif body, heavy-serif display, mono kicker chip, mono byline / dateline, 1–3° seeded camera rotation) and an accompanying `SurfaceRenderer.shaderPass?:` declarative field (extending ADR-0005's `OverlayRenderer.shaderPass` pattern from the overlay layer to the surface layer). The shader pass — `newspaper-physics` — carries halftone dot screen at body sizes and sub-pixel ink bleed at glyph edges. `SurfaceContent` gains an optional `kicker` slot for the mono section-name chip; the byline reuses `author`, the dateline reuses `dateLabel`, the headline reuses `title`, and body is left as an empty `AnnotationBodyBlock[]` for chapter-divider use cases.

## Considered options

- **Make newspaper a variant of `paper`** (rejected: substrate physics are categorically different — `paper` carries laid-paper grain + isotropic warmth, `newspaper` carries halftone + ink bleed + condensed-serif body. A "variant" flag on `paper` would mean two divergent shader code paths inside one Pipeline that share nothing meaningful; the registry's per-variant Pipeline grain is what keeps shaders self-contained).
- **Bake halftone + ink bleed into `effects.surface` as an EffectRenderer** (rejected: effects are deliberately scene-content-agnostic post-process passes. Newspaper physics needs to know "this is the newspaper substrate" — specifically that mid-tone luminance should screen but black ink and warm paper shouldn't. Forcing the effect runtime to plumb per-surface semantic state reverses the "effects are pure post-process" rule in `engine-architecture.md`).
- **Imperative `render(ctx)` on `SurfaceRenderer`** (rejected: same asymmetry argument ADR-0005 made for overlays — an imperative escape hatch alongside the rest of the declarative `*Renderer` registry would bite later, and `newspaper` is the canonical 95 %-case the declarative shape covers).
- **Add a separate Block type for newsprint text** (rejected: the substrate physics affect the whole card — kicker chip, headline, byline, body — not just one Block type. Surface is the correct layer per the `Layer` definition in `docs/CONTEXT.md`).
- **Put `kicker` on the `OverlayRenderer`'s content instead of `SurfaceContent`** (rejected: the newspaper kicker is a structural slot on the surface — it sits in the surface's HTML hierarchy and the surface's tear-on enter wipe carries it — not a standalone overlay positioned independently in canvas space).

## Consequences

`SurfaceTypeSchema` gains `'newspaper'`, `SurfaceContentSchema` gains optional `kicker`, an `isNewspaperSurface()` type guard joins the existing `isPaperSurface` / `isPlainSurface` set, and `Workspace.svelte`'s surface dispatch routes `newspaper` to the paper compositor (identical focal-slot + marks + drop-shadow scaffolding — the runtime difference lives entirely in the CanvasSource HTML/CSS and the declarative `shaderPass`). The `shaderPass` field is the same v1 contract ADR-0005 captured for overlays: a single fragment pass with self-contained uniforms, no cross-layer reads. The compose-pipeline invocation that *runs* the surface shader pass between DOM upload and final composite is a follow-up that ships alongside the equivalent overlay-side wiring per ADR-0005 — until then the declarative shape is the contract, identical to the state ADR-0005 currently sits in for overlays. First consumer of this Surface is `src/lib/presets/segment-title-card.json`, the chapter-divider Preset; future newspaper-shaped presets (op-eds, classifieds, headline-as-pullquote) compose from the same Pipeline.
