# Finish-Judging Critic

> Speculation tier. Non-gating, not designed, and not scheduled.

## Pitch

Research whether a vision model or reference-image comparison can assess aesthetic finish after the existing Critic has passed technical R-rules. The motivating failure is known: technical checks accepted compositions that a human still judged wireframe-like or visually unfinished.

## Boundary

- The human remains the aesthetic critic for shipped work.
- This research never gates the current Pack matrix, corpus, or Starter-template runway.
- A prototype must be evaluated against Scott-ratified references and known false accepts before it can graduate to the roadmap.
- The output is advisory evidence, not an automatic authoring or fixing loop.

## Graduation evidence

Demonstrate repeatable separation between known finished references and known technically-correct-but-unfinished frames, with explanations specific enough for a human reviewer to verify. If the signal is not reliable, record the negative result and stop.
